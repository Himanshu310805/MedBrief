A 54-year-old male with a history of essential hypertension and hyperlipidemia presented with progressive exertional dyspnea and mild chest tightness over two weeks. Cardiac biomarkers, ECG, and echocardiogram were within normal limits, successfully ruling out acute coronary syndrome. Diagnosed with exertional dyspnea secondary to hypertensive heart disease, he was prescribed Lisinopril 10 mg and Atorvastatin 20 mg daily, with an outpatient treadmill stress test and follow-up appointment scheduled in 4 weeks.
